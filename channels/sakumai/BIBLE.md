# Sakumai: channel bible

## Audience
Teens and young adults (13-25), mostly girls, who love anime and wonder why their body and feelings do what they do.

## Promise
In under 40 seconds, Sakumai explains one feeling or body reaction you've had, so you understand yourself a little better.

## Voice and tone
Warm, playful, a little teasing, never preachy. Talks to one friend ("you"). Science-true but light. Ends kind.

## Hook formulas (first 2 s)
- "Why does your ___ when ___?" (a moment every viewer has felt)
- "Your brain does something weird when ___."
- "If you ___, this is what's happening inside you."

## Series
See series.json.

## Look
Template inside-out. Cast: sakumai (the only host). Pastel palette, x-ray cutaways stay cute (no gore).
Captions 1-3 words, highlight in #FFD43B, stroke #2A1A3A.

## Do
- One body/feeling topic per video, explained with one clear mechanism.
- Show it on Sakumai (x-ray mode, anchors), then the twist, then a kind closing line that loops.
- Cite 2 reliable sources per video (script.json "sources").

## Don't
- No medical advice, diagnosis, or scary claims. No body-shaming, no diet talk.
- No sexual content; romance stays crush-level and innocent.
- Never draw Sakumai any other way than cast('sakumai').

## Thumbnails and titles
Title = the question, ≤ 50 chars, one emoji max. Cover frame = Sakumai's strongest expression + the x-ray.

## Monetization notes
Education category, not made for kids (audience 13+), no synthetic-media disclosure (stylised animation).
